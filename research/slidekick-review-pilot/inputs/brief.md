# Brief: Northstar Demo, Q3 field update (synthetic)

This is the original brief for a synthetic six-slide deck used in a review-regression pilot. The company, the product and every number are fictional.

- **Objective:** a short Q3 update for the fictional company Northstar Demo, which runs field sensors.
- **Audience:** an internal leadership meeting, presented live.
- **Length and mode:** 6 slides, presented mode.
- **Brand:** primary blue #2E4BD8, ink #14213D, amber #F2A541 (one highlight per slide). Inter for everything.
- **Content:**
  1. Title: "Q3 field update", with a line saying the deck is synthetic.
  2. Statement: sensor uptime held above 99% all quarter.
  3. Chart: monthly sensor readings, Jul 120k, Aug 190k, Sep 290k (2.4× from July to September), as a native column chart.
  4. Three key numbers: 99.3% uptime, 41 sites live, 6 h median repair time.
  5. Sites by region as a table: North 17, Central 14, South 10 (41 in total).
  6. Closing: the plan to add 20 sites by Q1, shown as a three-step timeline (Oct, Nov, Dec), with the brand's card-stack motif.
